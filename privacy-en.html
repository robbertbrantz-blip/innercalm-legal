Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 27, 2026

This privacy policy is designed to comply with Apple App Store Review Guidelines 5.1.1(i) and 5.1.2(i), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

Innercalm uses OpenAI's GPT service to power AI-driven features. This policy explains exactly what data is shared, how it is used, and how you remain in control.

1. Introduction

Innercalm ("we," "our," or "the App") is a wellness and mindfulness application that helps users reflect on their emotions, track their mental wellbeing, and engage in guided self-reflection through AI-powered conversations.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains in clear, specific terms what personal data we collect, how we collect it, what we do with it, and with whom we share it — including our use of third-party artificial intelligence (AI) services.

4. How We Store Your Data

Your personal data is stored locally on your device. If you have iCloud enabled, your data may sync via iCloud to your other Apple devices. We do not store your personal data on our servers.

4.1 Local Storage

All chat messages, journal entries, mood data, and conversation summaries are stored locally on your device using secure on-device storage. This means your data stays with you and is not uploaded to any server controlled by us.

4.2 iCloud Sync

If you have iCloud enabled on your device, your Innercalm data may be synced across your Apple devices via Apple's iCloud service. This sync is governed by Apple's own privacy policy and terms. We do not have access to your iCloud-stored data. You can disable iCloud sync for Innercalm in your device's Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

4.3 Data Retention

Because your data is stored locally (and optionally in iCloud), you have full control over retention. You can delete your data at any time by deleting the app or clearing app data in your device settings. Conversation data sent to OpenAI for processing is not stored by Innercalm on any server after the response is received.

5. Third-Party AI Service: OpenAI

IMPORTANT: Innercalm sends certain personal data to OpenAI's GPT service to provide AI-powered features. This section explains exactly what is shared, why, and what protections are in place.

5.1 What Data Is Sent to OpenAI

When you use AI-powered features in Innercalm, the following data may be transmitted to OpenAI's API:

  • Your chat messages: the text you type during an AI conversation session
  • Your mood and emotion data: the mood ratings or emotional descriptors you have provided, to give the AI context for a more relevant response
  • Conversation summaries: summaries generated from your prior conversation history, so the AI can maintain context across sessions

5.2 Who Receives This Data

Provider: OpenAI, L.L.C.
Service: OpenAI API (GPT language model)
Location: United States
Website: https://openai.com

5.3 Purpose of Data Sharing

Data is sent to OpenAI solely for the following purposes:

  • To generate personalized AI responses to your messages and reflections
  • To provide contextually relevant wellness guidance based on your emotional state and conversation history
  • To create conversation summaries that improve the continuity of your experience

5.4 OpenAI's Data Handling

Aspect Details
Data used for model training? No. We use OpenAI's API with data usage policies that ensure your data is NOT used to train or improve OpenAI's models.
Data retention by OpenAI OpenAI may retain API inputs for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, after which it is deleted. OpenAI does not store your data for longer-term purposes.
Encryption in transit Yes. All data transmitted to OpenAI is encrypted using TLS (Transport Layer Security).
Data Processing Agreement Our use of OpenAI's API is governed by OpenAI's Data Processing Agreement (DPA) and API Terms of Use, which provide protections equivalent to or exceeding those described in this policy.
Sub-processors OpenAI may use sub-processors (such as Microsoft Azure) to provide its services. Details are available in OpenAI's privacy documentation.

5.5 Your Consent

Before any personal data is sent to OpenAI, Innercalm will present you with a clear consent screen that:

  • Explains that your data will be sent to OpenAI's GPT service
  • Describes what specific data will be shared (chat messages, mood data, conversation summaries)
  • Requires you to explicitly opt in by tapping "Continue with AI" before any data is transmitted
  • Allows you to decline and still use non-AI features of the app

You can withdraw your consent at any time through the app's Settings. If you withdraw consent, no further data will be sent to OpenAI.

6. Data We Do Not Share or Sell

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics companies.

The only third party that receives personal data from Innercalm is OpenAI, solely for the purpose of powering the AI features as described in Section 5, and only after you have given explicit consent.

8. Children's Privacy

Innercalm is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU/EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

9. International Data Transfers

When you use AI features, your data is transmitted to OpenAI's servers, which are located in the United States. If you are located in the EU/EEA, this constitutes an international data transfer. This transfer is conducted under appropriate safeguards, including OpenAI's Data Processing Agreement and Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as applicable.

10. Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data:

  • All data stored locally on your device is protected by your device's security features (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID)
  • All data transmitted to OpenAI is encrypted using TLS during transit
  • We use OpenAI's API with enterprise-grade data handling policies that prevent your data from being used for model training
  • We do not maintain any server-side databases containing your personal data
